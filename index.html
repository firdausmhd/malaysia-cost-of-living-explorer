Inflation has cooled every year since 2022

The annual rate fell from 3.3% to 2.5%, then 1.8%, then 1.4%. Monthly readings in 2026 have stayed near 1.8% to 1.9%.

Slower does not mean cheaper

Prices kept climbing each year, so the same basket of goods now costs about 9% more than it did in 2021.

Services and finance now lead

In 2025 personal care, insurance and restaurants rose fastest. Communication was the one big category getting cheaper, down 4.3%.

About this report

Figures come from the Department of Statistics Malaysia (DOSM) annual consumer price index analyses, published as open data on OpenDOSM under a CC BY 4.0 licence. Values were keyed in from those releases and the index changes were checked against the stated percentages.

  • Category figures use DOSM's 13-division classification, which applies from 2024. Earlier years used a 12-division version with different names, so categories are compared for 2024 and 2025 only.
  • The 2026 monthly readings are year-on-year rates reported in the press and are not directly comparable with annual averages.
  • To refresh the numbers, pull the cpi_annual_inflation dataset from OpenDOSM.

Sources: DOSM annual CPI 2025, 2024, 2023, 2022, OpenDOSM catalogue, The Star, September 2026, Malay Mail, August 2026.
